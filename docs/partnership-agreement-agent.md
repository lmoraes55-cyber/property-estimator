## 1. Background
1.1 GroundWorks operates a rental-intelligence platform that helps Dubai property owners decide between short-term and long-term rental and, where long-term rental is the better fit, introduces those owners to vetted leasing agents.

1.2 The Agent provides residential long-term leasing services in Dubai and wishes to receive qualified owner introductions ("Leads") from GroundWorks on the terms below.

## 2. Definitions
2.1 **"Lead"** — an owner introduction generated by GroundWorks and delivered to the Agent, identified by a unique **GroundWorks Reference** (format `GW-XXXX-XXXX`), including the owner's contact details and property context.

2.2 **"Qualified Lead"** — a Lead that meets the criteria in Schedule A (genuine owner, contactable details, property in the Agent's service area).

2.3 **"Conversion" / "Signed Lease"** — a tenancy for which the owner of a Lead, introduced by GroundWorks, enters into a leasing engagement with the Agent and the Agent successfully secures a signed tenancy contract (Ejari) for the property within the Attribution Window (Schedule A).

2.4 **"Leasing Commission"** — the commission the Agent earns from the leasing transaction (typically a percentage of the annual rent, or a fixed amount).

## 3. Scope of the Partnership
3.1 GroundWorks will refer Qualified Leads to the Agent. Each Lead is referred **exclusively** to the Agent named in it unless stated otherwise.

3.2 GroundWorks makes **no guarantee** as to the number, quality, or conversion of Leads. GroundWorks does not act as the Agent's agent, does not broker tenancies, and is not a party to any tenancy contract.

3.3 The Agent is solely responsible for its own services, RERA compliance, tenancy paperwork (incl. Ejari), pricing, and contracts with owners and tenants.

## 4. Fees & Payment
4.1 **Success-based only.** GroundWorks is paid **only when a Lead converts** into a Signed Lease. There is **no per-lead charge** and **no fee for Leads that do not convert**. For each Conversion, the Agent shall pay GroundWorks the **Success Fee** set out in **Schedule A**.

4.2 The Agent shall report each Conversion (referencing the GroundWorks Reference) within **[5] business days** of the tenancy being signed, including the Leasing Commission earned so the Success Fee can be calculated. GroundWorks may also confirm Conversions directly with the owner.

4.3 GroundWorks will invoice the Success Fee on Conversion. Invoices are payable within **[14] days**. All fees are exclusive of **VAT**, which will be added where applicable.

4.4 Late payments may accrue interest at **[1]%** per month on overdue amounts.

## 5. Non-Circumvention
5.1 For each Lead, the Agent shall not, for **[12] months** from delivery, engage the referred owner (or that owner's property) **outside** this Agreement in order to avoid fees, whether directly or via an affiliate, employee, or related party.

5.2 A Conversion arising from a Lead is subject to the fees in Schedule A regardless of the contracting entity or individual agent used. **‹INITIAL FIELD›**

## 6. Lead Data, Consent & Data Protection
6.1 Owners consent to having their details shared with a matched partner for the purpose of an introduction. The Agent shall use Lead data **only** to contact and serve that owner, and not for unrelated marketing or resale.

6.2 Each Party shall comply with applicable UAE data-protection law (incl. Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection, "PDPL") and keep Lead data secure.

6.3 On termination, or on an owner's request, the Agent shall stop using and (where required) delete Lead data for any owner that did not become a client.

## 7. Quality, Conduct & Brand
7.1 The Agent shall respond to Leads promptly and professionally and represent GroundWorks introductions accurately.

7.2 The Agent shall not use GroundWorks' name, logo, or marks without prior written consent, except to acknowledge the referral to the introduced owner.

7.3 GroundWorks may suspend Lead delivery if the Agent's service quality, owner complaints, RERA standing, or payment status warrant it.

## 8. Term & Termination
8.1 This Agreement starts on the signature date and continues until terminated.

8.2 Either Party may terminate on **[30] days'** written notice. Either Party may terminate immediately for material breach not remedied within **[14] days** of notice.

8.3 Termination does not affect fees due for Conversions occurring before termination, or the Non-Circumvention obligations in clause 5.

## 9. Confidentiality
9.1 Each Party shall keep the other's non-public information (including Lead data, pricing, and commercial terms) confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 10. Liability
10.1 Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

10.2 Subject to 10.1, neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, and each Party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the **fees paid/payable in the [6] months** before the claim.

10.3 GroundWorks provides Leads "as is" and is not liable for an owner's decisions, an owner's failure to proceed, or the outcome of any engagement between the Agent and an owner.

## 11. Independent Parties
11.1 The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing creates a partnership (in the legal sense), joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

## 12. Governing Law & Disputes
12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Emirate of Dubai and the applicable federal laws of the United Arab Emirates.

12.2 The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **[Dubai Courts / DIFC Courts]**. The Parties will first attempt to resolve disputes amicably.

## 13. General
13.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions.

13.2 Variations must be in writing and signed by both Parties. If any clause is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
