## 1. Background
1.1 GroundWorks operates a rental-intelligence platform that helps Dubai property owners decide between short-term (STR) and long-term rental and, where appropriate, introduces those owners to vetted holiday-home operators.

1.2 The Partner provides short-term rental / holiday-home management services in Dubai and wishes to receive qualified owner introductions ("Leads") from GroundWorks on the terms below.

## 2. Definitions
2.1 **"Lead"** — an owner introduction generated by GroundWorks and delivered to the Partner, identified by a unique **GroundWorks Reference** (format `GW-XXXX-XXXX`), including the owner's contact details and property/analysis context.

2.2 **"Qualified Lead"** — a Lead that meets the criteria in Schedule A (e.g. genuine owner, contactable details, property in the Partner's service area).

2.3 **"Conversion" / "Signed Property"** — a property for which the owner of a Lead enters into a management agreement (or equivalent engagement) with the Partner within the Attribution Window (Schedule A).

2.4 **"Management Fee"** — the fee the Partner charges the owner for managing the property (e.g. a percentage of rental revenue).

## 3. Scope of the Partnership
3.1 GroundWorks will refer Qualified Leads to the Partner. Each Lead is referred **exclusively** to the Partner named in it unless stated otherwise.

3.2 GroundWorks makes **no guarantee** as to the number, quality, or conversion of Leads. GroundWorks does not act as the Partner's agent and does not manage properties.

3.3 The Partner is solely responsible for its own services, pricing, contracts with owners, licensing (incl. DET/DTCM holiday-home permits), and compliance.

## 4. Fees & Payment
4.1 **Success-based only.** GroundWorks is paid **only when a Lead converts** into a Signed Property. There is **no per-lead charge** and **no fee for Leads that do not convert**. For each Conversion, the Partner shall pay GroundWorks the **Success Fee** set out in **Schedule A**.

4.2 The Partner shall report each Conversion (referencing the GroundWorks Reference) within **[5] business days** of signing the owner, including the agreed Management Fee / rate so the Success Fee can be calculated. GroundWorks may also confirm Conversions directly with the owner.

4.3 GroundWorks will invoice the Success Fee on Conversion (and, where the fee is income-based, in line with Schedule A). Invoices are payable within **[14] days**. All fees are exclusive of **VAT**, which will be added where applicable.

4.4 Late payments may accrue interest at **[1]%** per month on overdue amounts.

## 5. Non-Circumvention
5.1 For each Lead, the Partner shall not, for **[12] months** from delivery, engage the referred owner (or that owner's property) **outside** this Agreement in order to avoid fees, whether directly or via an affiliate, employee, or related party.

5.2 A Conversion arising from a Lead is subject to the fees in Schedule A regardless of the contracting entity used by the Partner. **‹INITIAL FIELD›**

## 6. Lead Data, Consent & Data Protection
6.1 Owners consent to having their details shared with a matched partner for the purpose of an introduction. The Partner shall use Lead data **only** to contact and serve that owner, and not for unrelated marketing or resale.

6.2 Each Party shall comply with applicable UAE data-protection law (incl. Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection, "PDPL") and keep Lead data secure.

6.3 On termination, or on an owner's request, the Partner shall stop using and (where required) delete Lead data for any owner that did not become a client.

## 7. Quality, Conduct & Brand
7.1 The Partner shall respond to Leads promptly and professionally and represent GroundWorks introductions accurately.

7.2 The Partner shall not use GroundWorks' name, logo, or marks without prior written consent, except to acknowledge the referral to the introduced owner.

7.3 GroundWorks may suspend Lead delivery if the Partner's service quality, owner complaints, or payment status warrant it.

## 8. Term & Termination
8.1 This Agreement starts on the signature date and continues until terminated.

8.2 Either Party may terminate on **[30] days'** written notice. Either Party may terminate immediately for material breach not remedied within **[14] days** of notice.

8.3 Termination does not affect fees due for Leads delivered or Conversions occurring before termination, or the Non-Circumvention obligations in clause 5.

## 9. Confidentiality
9.1 Each Party shall keep the other's non-public information (including Lead data, pricing, and commercial terms) confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 10. Liability
10.1 Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

10.2 Subject to 10.1, neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, and each Party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the **fees paid/payable in the [6] months** before the claim.

10.3 GroundWorks provides Leads "as is" and is not liable for an owner's decisions, an owner's failure to proceed, or the outcome of any engagement between the Partner and an owner.

## 11. Independent Parties
11.1 The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing creates a partnership (in the legal sense), joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

## 12. Governing Law & Disputes
12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Emirate of Dubai and the applicable federal laws of the United Arab Emirates.

12.2 The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **[Dubai Courts / DIFC Courts]**. The Parties will first attempt to resolve disputes amicably.

## 13. General
13.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions.

13.2 Variations must be in writing and signed by both Parties. If any clause is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
